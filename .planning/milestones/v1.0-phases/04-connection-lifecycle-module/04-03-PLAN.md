<objective>
Make the domain-claim primitive safe under Postgres concurrency: one organization-scoped advisory lock around every claim, release and release-all, and owner rows acquired in sorted order with the refused domain still named in input order.

Purpose: RESEARCH proved two deadlocks on Postgres 16.16 (update vs remove, Experiment 1; opposite-order claims across organizations, Experiment 4) and that flipping the order alone orphans rows (Experiment 2). The lock is the half of the fix that lives in the store primitive (ADR-EA-041 decision 9, `SqlStore.atomically`); the other half, claims-first ordering in every operation, is the lifecycle service (04-04, adopted by OIDC in 04-05). CONN-03 atomicity and the CONN-05 race oracle depend on both.

Output: DomainClaims.ts lock and sorted acquisition; a Postgres lock-key test; additive concurrent contract cases; race case 2 green.
</objective>
