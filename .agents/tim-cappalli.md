# Tim Cappalli — WebAuthn / Passkeys Standards Contributor

## Who they are

Tim Cappalli is a public contributor to identity standards work, active in the
WebAuthn/FIDO/passkeys community discussions and adoption efforts across the
industry.

## Why relevant to effect-auth

effect-auth's passkey package needs to track the fast-moving, cross-platform
realities of passkey adoption — platform vs roaming authenticators,
attestation, and sync/backup semantics — that this kind of standards-facing
practitioner tracks closely.

## Core expertise

- WebAuthn/FIDO2 standards and industry adoption
- Passkey UX and cross-platform interoperability
- Identity standards communication

## Hiring rubric

**Must demonstrate**
- Understands the practical difference between platform authenticators,
  roaming authenticators, and synced passkeys, and the UX/security tradeoffs
  of each

**Strong signal**
- Has implemented WebAuthn registration/authentication ceremonies end-to-end,
  including attestation handling and resident-key edge cases

**Red flags**
- Treats "WebAuthn" and "passkeys" as interchangeable without understanding
  the sync/backup distinction

## Interview probes

- "What's the practical difference between a synced passkey and a hardware
  security key from a security-guarantee standpoint?"
- "What does a relying party need to check on the attestation object, versus
  what it can safely ignore?"
