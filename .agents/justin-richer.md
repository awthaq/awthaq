# Justin Richer — OAuth2/OIDC Contributor, Co-author of "OAuth 2 in Action"

## Who they are

Justin Richer is a contributor to OAuth2/OpenID Connect standards work and
co-author of "OAuth 2 in Action," and has been involved in follow-on
authorization protocol work such as GNAP (Grant Negotiation and Authorization
Protocol).

## Why relevant to effect-auth

Understanding not just how OAuth2 works but why its design has known
limitations (which GNAP attempts to address) helps evaluate effect-auth's own
token/grant design choices with full awareness of the tradeoffs, instead of
"because that's how OAuth2 does it."

## Core expertise

- OAuth2/OIDC protocol internals
- Next-generation authorization protocol design (GNAP)
- Teaching protocol-level security concepts clearly

## Hiring rubric

**Must demonstrate**
- Can explain a specific limitation of OAuth2's grant model that a newer
  protocol effort was designed to address

**Strong signal**
- Has implemented an OAuth2 authorization server (not just a client) and can
  discuss its edge cases from direct experience

**Red flags**
- Treats OAuth2 as a finished, uncriticizable standard rather than one with
  known, documented tradeoffs

## Interview probes

- "What's a real limitation of OAuth2's grant model that a protocol like GNAP
  tries to solve?"
- "What's the hardest edge case in implementing token refresh correctly?"
