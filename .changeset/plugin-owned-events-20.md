`@awthaq/admin` owns its events (ADR-EA-049): the nine definitions `auth.admin.{impersonationStarted, impersonationStopped, userUpdated, sessionRevoked, userBanned, userUnbanned, userDeleted, userEmailChangeRequested, userPasswordSet}` moved from `@awthaq/core` to `AdminEvents`, are declared on the plugins' `events`, and are published with `AuthEvents.emit`. Tags, payloads, actors, personal-data fields and attempt lanes are unchanged. `Admin` declares six (`AdminEvents.events`) and `AdminAccounts`, composable without `Admin`, declares the three it publishes (`AdminEvents.accountsEvents`). `ImpersonationOwnerNotice` subscribes with `AuthEvents.on(AdminEvents.AdminImpersonationStartedEvent, …)`. A row these definitions write records `meta.pii: ["reason"]` for the impersonation and ban events; rows written before keep falling back to `AuditLog.PRE_ADR_049_PII`. `auth.admin.{impersonationDenied, actionDenied, seeded, seedRefused}` stay in core; `auth.admin.organization{Suspended, Unsuspended}` move with `@awthaq/organization`'s events.

Migration: `AuthEvents.AdminImpersonationStartedEvent`, `AuthEvents.AdminImpersonationStoppedEvent`, `AuthEvents.AdminUserUpdatedEvent`, `AuthEvents.AdminSessionRevokedEvent`, `AuthEvents.AdminUserBannedEvent`, `AuthEvents.AdminUserUnbannedEvent`, `AuthEvents.AdminUserDeletedEvent`, `AuthEvents.AdminUserEmailChangeRequestedEvent` and `AuthEvents.AdminUserPasswordSetEvent` → `AdminEvents.<same name>` from `@awthaq/admin`. `AuthEvents.AuthEventTag`/`AuthEvent` no longer include these nine tags: subscribe with `AuthEvents.on(AdminEvents.AdminImpersonationStartedEvent, …)`, narrow the stream with `AuthEvents.is(AdminEvents.AdminImpersonationStartedEvent)`, decode an audit row with `AuditLog.payloadAs(AdminEvents.AdminImpersonationStartedEvent)`, and replace `AuthEvents.payloadOf(e)` with `Schema.decodeUnknownSync(AdminEvents.AdminImpersonationStartedEvent)(e)`. ADR-EA-049, BEH-EA-101.
